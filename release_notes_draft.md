# Release Notes Draft

## Title

Version 24: Performance & Peace

## Body

### AI Performance Boost

The game's AI has been significantly optimized to reduce lag and stutter, especially in large, late-game scenarios with many bots. You should experience much smoother gameplay.

### New! Protected Start

You can now add a peace timer (e.g., 5, 10, or 15 minutes) to single-player and private lobby games. During this time, no attacks are allowed, giving everyone a chance to build up their economy and defenses before the action starts. You can find the option in the game setup menu.
